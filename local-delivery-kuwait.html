FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver same-day in Kuwait?
Yes — same-day delivery is available across Kuwait for orders placed within our cut-off times, with next-day as a standard option for everything else.
Do you offer cash on delivery (COD)?
Yes. We collect COD on your behalf and settle it back to you with clear reporting, so reconciliation is simple.
Which areas of Kuwait do you cover?
We deliver across all governorates — Al Asimah, Hawalli, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, Jahra, and Mubarak Al-Kabeer.
